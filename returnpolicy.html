Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Returns and Exchanges

If you receive a damaged or incorrect furniture item, please inform us within 7 days of delivery for a replacement or return request.

2. Eligibility Criteria

Items must be unused and in their original packaging condition. Custom-made items are non-refundable unless defective upon arrival.

3. Refund Process

Once inspected and approved, refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
